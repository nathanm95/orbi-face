# Orbi: store text

## Short description (`pebble publish --description`)

A little orb with big feelings: weather, hats and faces

## Full description

Meet Orbi, a glossy little orb who lives on your wrist and has a lot of
feelings about it.

Every couple of minutes he puts on a show. He laughs until he cries, winks
with his tongue out, falls in love with heart eyes, sneezes, gets dizzy, rolls
his eyes, whistles a tune, hiccups, and drops a pair of shades when he's
feeling cool. On the hour he throws a party, hat and confetti included.

He knows the weather where you are. When it rains, a cloud soaks him until he
gets his umbrella out. In snow he pulls on a beanie and catches flakes on his
tongue. He shivers in the cold, pants in the heat, squints into fog and jumps
at thunder. He wears a witch hat for Halloween and a Santa hat in December.

Flick your wrist or tap the screen to poke him. Poke him in the eye and he
flinches; anywhere else tickles.

Settings:
- 12 or 24 hour clock, on a black pill or plain
- Six date formats, with the temperature in °C or °F
- Battery percentage: always, only when low, or hidden
- Five colours: classic yellow, tangerine, bubblegum, mint and sky
- Liveliness, from never moving to always moving
- Battery saver: goes still below a battery level you choose
- Quiet hours: he puts on his nightcap and sleeps
- Props, seasonal hats and the hourly party can each be turned off

Orbi only draws frames while he's moving, so a still Orbi costs no more
battery than any other watchface.

Weather comes from Open-Meteo using your phone's location.

For the Pebble Round 2.
